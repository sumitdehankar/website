Contact

If your team is dealing with a backend problem that keeps consuming engineering time without a clear explanation, I'd be happy to discuss it.

Typical situations include APIs becoming slower over time, production incidents that take too long to diagnose, deployments creating unexpected instability, observability gaps that make debugging difficult, or backend systems that have become harder to operate confidently.

Initial conversations are informal and focused on understanding the problem, available technical context, and whether the engagement is a good fit.

Examples of Problems I Can Help With

  • API latency increased but the bottleneck is still unclear
  • Production incidents require hours of investigation every time
  • Deployments regularly introduce operational risk or instability
  • Background jobs are creating unexpected load or contention
  • Logs and metrics exist but still don't explain what is happening
  • Teams are spending too much time firefighting recurring issues
  • Operational workflows have become difficult to maintain confidently

How Initial Discussions Work

Most conversations begin with a description of the problem, the systems involved, and what your team has already tried.

If the situation appears to be a good fit, we can discuss a focused diagnosis engagement and whether follow-up improvement assistance would be useful afterward.

Async communication is preferred whenever possible.
