How Engagement Works

Engagements are intentionally kept lightweight, scoped, and operationally focused.

1. Initial Discussion

Share current operational concerns, backend pain points, and investigation goals.

2. Scope Alignment

Define investigation focus areas, expected deliverables, timelines, and communication expectations.

3. Investigation

Async review of provided metrics, logs, workflows, traces, and operational visibility.

4. Findings Summary

Receive prioritized observations, operational insights, and practical improvement recommendations.

5. Optional Follow-Up

Clarification discussions or limited implementation guidance if additional context is needed.
