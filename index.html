Frequently Asked Questions

Do you implement fixes?

My primary focus is understanding backend operational problems and helping teams identify the most effective improvements. Depending on the engagement, I may provide implementation guidance, review proposed solutions, and help evaluate tradeoffs. I do not operate as an embedded engineer or take ownership of implementation work.

What kinds of problems are a good fit?

Situations where symptoms are clear but root causes are not. Examples include unexplained latency increases, recurring production incidents, deployment instability, observability gaps, debugging bottlenecks, and backend workflows that have become difficult to operate or maintain.

Do you need access to our production systems?

Not necessarily. Many engagements begin using existing logs, metrics, traces, architecture information, deployment history, and operational context provided by the engineering team. Any additional access requirements would be discussed before work begins.

How do engagements usually work?

Engagements begin with a discussion of the problem, followed by scope alignment and diagnosis. Findings and recommendations are delivered first. If helpful, a separate improvement assistance phase can follow.

Do you work synchronously with teams?

Engagements are primarily async-first. This keeps collaboration flexible and allows teams to provide information without introducing unnecessary meeting overhead.

What technologies do you work with?

My professional background is primarily in .NET backend systems, but the focus of this practice is operational reasoning, diagnosis, reliability, and backend workflows rather than a specific technology stack.

What happens after I contact you?

We'll discuss the problem, determine whether it is a good fit, and decide whether a scoped engagement makes sense. There is no obligation to move forward after the initial discussion.

Pricing Guidance

Every engagement is scoped individually based on the problem, available information, investigation complexity, and expected level of involvement.

Some situations can be reviewed in a focused engagement lasting a few days. Others require deeper analysis across multiple systems, workflows, or operational areas.

Engagements are typically structured in two phases:

  • Diagnosis & Findings – understanding the problem, identifying likely causes, and providing prioritized recommendations.
  • Improvement Assistance (Optional) – helping teams evaluate and apply practical improvements after findings have been delivered.

Pricing is discussed after an initial conversation and scope review. This helps ensure recommendations and expectations are aligned before work begins.

Diagnostic engagements typically start from ₹20,000. More complex investigations involving multiple systems, teams, or operational workflows are scoped separately based on the level of analysis required.

If you're unsure whether your situation is a good fit, the best place to start is a short introductory discussion.
